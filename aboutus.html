About Us

We opened our first Kota Joe in Sunnyside Pretoria, marking the beginning of our exciting journey. Soon after this we opened Gandhi Square Johannesburg.

Kota Joe Edenvale

Kota Joe Edenvale Roadhouse can be found in the heart of Edenvale town. Our first roadhouse where we took the old roadhouse concept and brought in a new fresh modern feel but still keeping the nostalgia with trays on car windows, vibey music playing and entertainment on big screens. Recently renovated in 2018.
